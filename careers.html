Job Opportunities

Vederra Modular seeks skilled tradespeople, including carpenters, electricians, plumbers, and drywall installers, to build modular homes in a controlled factory setting. Responsibilities include precision work, team collaboration, and adherence to strict quality and safety standards. This role offers year-round employment benefits over traditional outdoor construction.

Apprentice Program

A hands-on program for motivated individuals to gain technical skills and industry knowledge in modular housing. Participants receive training and mentorship, with structured career advancement and competitive wages.
