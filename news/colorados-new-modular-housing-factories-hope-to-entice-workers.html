Colorado's New Modular Housing Factories Hope to Entice Workers

May 13 · Written By Jason Davis

Vederra is excited to begin our upcoming venture into Colorado's modular housing landscape. After years of immersion in various facets of modular housing, we are proud to spearhead Vederra's journey, poised to open our state-of-the-art factory in Aurora. With a strategic location chosen near Aurora and Commerce City, we aim to leverage the existing talent pool for our workforce, recognizing the pivotal role skilled labor plays in our industry.

Our commitment to quality extends beyond our products; it encompasses our workforce as well. We are dedicated to offering competitive wages, starting at $29 per hour, along with comprehensive benefits, to attract and retain skilled professionals. Furthermore, we are collaborating with BuildStrong Academy to provide educational opportunities at our facility, nurturing the next generation of construction trades workers while addressing the industry's shortage in Colorado.

At Vederra, we see modular construction not just as a method but as a solution to the challenges faced by traditional building practices. By offering a sheltered environment and consistent workflow, we provide our employees with stability and opportunities for growth. While we anticipate challenges such as potential turnover, our vision is to cultivate a company culture centered around supporting Colorado's affordable housing needs, fostering employee engagement and retention in the process.

Our commitment to excellence drives us forward as we prepare to launch our factory in Aurora. With a focus on innovation, sustainability, and community impact, Vederra is poised to make a lasting impression on Colorado's housing landscape, one modular unit at a time.
